Compensation models

How affiliates get paid.

Choose the model that fits your traffic — or combine both as your portfolio grows.

CPA

Cost per Acquisition

Receive a fixed amount for every new user who signs up through your link and meets the campaign criteria — such as a minimum deposit.

RevShare

Revenue Share

Earn a recurring percentage of the net revenue generated by the users you referred — you keep earning for as long as they stay active.
